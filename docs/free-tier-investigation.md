## Caveats the owner must accept or resolve
1. **Billing must not be attached.** A Gemini key from a Google Cloud project with a linked billing account is a paid tier; a Groq organisation upgraded to Developer is pay-as-you-go. Code cannot see Gemini's billing state. It does check Groq: responses must report the Free-plan header maxima (8,000 tokens/min, 1,000 requests/day) or the run aborts (`PaidTierDetected`). Nothing runs unless `FASHIONGRAPH_FREE_TIER_CONFIRMED=1` states the owner has checked both accounts.
2. **Gemini free-tier data use.** Google's terms say unpaid-service prompts and responses may be read by human reviewers and used to improve products, and that sensitive, confidential or personal information must not be submitted. Only the public, non-personal CNMI schedule text is sent. For users in the EEA, Switzerland or UK the terms also require paid services when *making API clients available to users*; this project runs a local research script and makes no client available. This is a reading of the terms, not legal advice: confirm it fits your situation.
3. **Gemini free limits are assumptions** (5 req/min, 50 req/day, 30,000 tokens/min, 100,000 tokens/day, deliberately low) until you replace them with the values on your AI Studio rate-limit page.
4. **Qwen3.8-27B is a Preview model**; Groq may change or withdraw it. If it disappears mid-project the run stops (model-mismatch / error) and no other model is substituted.
5. **Live smoke results (docs/smoke-results.md) settled:** served-model strings, `temperature 0`, `include_reasoning false`, `reasoning_effort none`/`low`, the Gemini `modelVersion`, and the Groq Free-plan header check. **Still unsettled:** Gemini's real free limits, whether Groq counts the output cap against tokens/min, whether the Groq Free plan needs a card (docs silent), and Gemini's 503 behaviour under load. Originally listed as not stated in the docs: whether Groq counts the output cap against tokens/min (the guard assumes it does); Groq's served-model strings and header values; acceptance of `temperature 0`, `include_reasoning false` and `reasoning_effort none` on these two models; whether the Free plan needs a card (the docs are silent); the exact Gemini `modelVersion`.
6. **Conditions are comparable, not identical.** Reasoning cannot be switched off uniformly: Qwen runs with none, gpt-oss-120b at its minimum (low), Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite at MINIMAL. Gemini's sampling cannot be pinned (no temperature). Output caps differ (400 / 1,500 / 1,024 tokens) because gpt-oss reasoning tokens consume the cap. The report must state these differences next to the results.
